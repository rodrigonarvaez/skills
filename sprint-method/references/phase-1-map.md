# Phase 1 — Map

**Goal:** Understand the problem space and choose one focused target to carry into Phase 2.

---

## Exercise 1: Long-Term Goal

Ask the user: *"Where do you want to be in 6 months to 5 years? Imagine the project succeeded — what does that look like?"*

Help them write a clear, optimistic long-term goal statement. Example format:
> "In [timeframe], [target customer] can [desired outcome] because of [solution]."

---

## Exercise 2: Sprint Questions

Flip the goal — ask the user to imagine the project **failed**. What went wrong?

Prompt: *"If this sprint fails, what are the most likely reasons? What assumptions could turn out to be wrong?"*

Reframe each failure as a question. These become the **Sprint Questions** — the specific uncertainties the sprint is trying to resolve.

Example:
- Fear: "Users don't trust us with their data" → Sprint Question: "Will users trust us with their data?"
- Fear: "The onboarding is too complex" → Sprint Question: "Can a new user complete onboarding without help?"

Aim for 3–6 sprint questions. Capture them as a numbered list.

---

## Exercise 3: Customer Journey Map

Build a simple journey map together. Use this structure:

```
Actors (left) → Steps (middle) → Outcome (right)
```

- **Actors:** Who is involved? (target customer, supporting characters)
- **Steps:** What does the customer do from first contact to achieving their goal? List 5–10 steps.
- **Outcome:** What does success look like for the customer?

Ask clarifying questions to fill in gaps. Represent the map as a structured list or table.

---

## Exercise 4: Ask the Experts

Claude takes the role of each sprint team member in turn and shares what they know — pain points, patterns, constraints, and relevant knowledge about the problem.

For each role, Claude contributes 2–3 observations. Then ask the user: *"What do you know that we haven't surfaced yet?"*

Capture any new insights that change or sharpen the journey map.

---

## Exercise 5: How Might We (HMW) Notes

During Ask the Experts, reframe problems as opportunities using "How Might We..." questions.

Prompt the user: *"Based on what we've discussed, what problems are you most eager to solve?"*

Generate 5–10 HMW notes from the conversation. Example:
- "How might we make onboarding feel effortless for first-time users?"
- "How might we build trust before asking for personal data?"

Group them into 2–4 themes. Present the themes and ask the user to vote: *"Which 1–2 themes feel most critical to address?"*

---

## Exercise 6: Pick a Target

The Decider (user) makes two choices:

1. **Target customer** — Which actor from the journey map are you designing for?
2. **Target moment** — Which step on the journey map is the most critical to get right?

Confirm these choices and document them clearly.

---

## Phase 1 Artifacts

Summarize and confirm with the user before proceeding:

1. **Long-term goal** — one sentence
2. **Sprint questions** — numbered list
3. **Customer journey map** — actors, steps, outcome
4. **Top HMW themes** — 2–4 clusters
5. **Target** — customer + critical moment
