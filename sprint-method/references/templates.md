## Interview Script

```
INTRO (5 min)
"Thanks for joining. We're testing some ideas today — there are no right or wrong answers,
and we're not testing you. We want to learn from your honest reactions. Okay if I take notes?"

CONTEXT QUESTIONS (10 min)
"Tell me about how you typically [relevant activity in problem space]."
"What's the hardest part about [problem area]?"
"Walk me through the last time you [relevant behavior]."

INTRODUCE PROTOTYPE (25–35 min)
"I'm going to show you something we've been working on. As you look at it, please think out loud
— tell me what you're noticing, what you're wondering, what you'd do next."
[Show prototype]
"What were you thinking there?"
"What would you do next?"
"What does this remind you of?"

DEBRIEF (5–10 min)
"What's your overall impression?"
"What surprised you?"
"If you were telling a friend about this, what would you say?"
"Is there anything you expected to see that wasn't there?"
```
