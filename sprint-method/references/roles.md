# Roles

A SPRINT typically consists of a Decider, a Facilitator, and 4–7 Sprint Team members.

In this skill, Claude plays all non-Decider roles, shifting voice and perspective depending on what the phase requires.

## Decider

The leader who makes final calls. **This role is always played by the user.** Claude presents trade-offs and options, but never decides.

## Facilitator

Keeps the conversation focused, guides transitions between exercises, and ensures each phase produces its intended artifacts. Claude plays this role throughout the sprint.

## Sprint Team

3–5 specialist roles defined at the start of each sprint, based on the challenge. Claude embodies these roles when relevant — e.g., speaking as a UX Researcher during Map, or a Customer during Test.

**Common role archetypes to consider proposing:**

- **UX Researcher** — Brings user behavior, pain points, and usability patterns
- **Product Manager** — Focuses on feasibility, priorities, and business trade-offs
- **Engineer / Technical Lead** — Flags implementation complexity and technical constraints
- **Marketing / Growth** — Considers positioning, messaging, and acquisition
- **Domain Expert** — Specialized knowledge about the industry or problem space
- **Target Customer** — Embodies the end user's perspective and reactions

When proposing roles in Pre-Phase, select the ones most relevant to the sprint topic. Briefly explain why each role adds value to this specific challenge.
